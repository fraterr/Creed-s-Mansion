Contact

Bugs, ideas, corrections

This is a one-person house, so the answer to most things is yes, I read it. Patches have bugs, write-ups have mistakes, and the good ideas for what to build next have mostly come from other people. If you have any of the three, the form is below.

What is especially welcome: a bug in one of the patches, because I cannot test every party composition and every battle. A correction, if you check something I published against your own copy of the ROM and it does not match. And an idea for a mod — the quality-of-life things you have wanted for thirty years and nobody built.

Before you write about a bug

A bug report gets fixed roughly ten times faster with four extra lines. If you can, tell me which patch and version you applied, which ROM you applied it to, what you were running it on, and what you saw. Something like this is perfect:

Patch:    Turn Queue & Tactics v15
ROM:      Shining Force (USA), unheadered
Running:  Fusion 3.64 on Windows
Happened: the turn bar stays on screen after the battle at Rudo
Expected: it disappears when the map loads

If it is something you can show, a screenshot or a savestate says more than a paragraph. Mention it in the message and I will tell you where to send it.

Send a message

The form is handled by Web3Forms, which passes the message to my inbox. Nothing is stored on this site and there are still no cookies or trackers here. Your address is used to reply to you and for nothing else.

What happens next

I answer in a few days, usually less. A confirmed bug in a patch goes into the next version, and the write-up for that mod gets a line in its changelog. A correction to an article gets applied and credited, because a page that quietly fixes itself is worth less than one that shows its working.

No ROMs are hosted on this site, and please do not send any. Bring your own copy of the game and the patches here will do the rest.
