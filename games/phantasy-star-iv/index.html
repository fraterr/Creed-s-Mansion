Phantasy Star IV

Sega Mega Drive · 1993

Phantasy Star IV: The End of the Millennium closed the Mega Drive's biggest role-playing series in December 1993 in Japan and early 1995 in the West. It is a turn-based game where the turns are decided by a die roll on top of agility, five fighters face up to five enemies, and the commands you queue can fuse into a Combination Attack if the order works out. Sega's manual promised fifteen of those.

The ROM is three megabytes, its script is Kosinski-compressed, and there is a complete disassembly that rebuilds it byte for byte, which makes it a good subject for the kind of reading this house does: not what the guides say, but what the code does.

Notes & teardowns

The Fifteenth Combination New

The manual says there are fifteen Combination Attacks and players have found fourteen. The tables in the ROM settle it, and on the way they show how a combo is actually detected: a four-slot window on the turn order, an enemy in the wrong place, and a combo whose whole window of opportunity is one dungeon long.
