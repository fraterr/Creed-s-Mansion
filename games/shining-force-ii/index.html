Shining Force II

Sega Mega Drive · 1993

Shining Force II arrived on the Mega Drive in 1993, built by Sonic! Software Planning — the studio that would become Camelot — and published by Sega. It took everything the first game did and gave it room: a larger world you cross on foot instead of through a menu, thirty-odd recruits, and battles that sprawl far enough that losing the left flank is a thing that can happen to you.

It is also the game this house is named after. Creed's Mansion is a real place in it, and the wizard who lives there is exactly the sort of person who would let you poke around in his things.

What goes in this room is not romhacks, at least not yet. It is teardowns: the systems underneath the game, read out of the ROM rather than inferred from playing. The first one is the dwarf smith, because thirty years of guides have been repeating the wrong odds about him.
