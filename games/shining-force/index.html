Shining Force

Sega Mega Drive · Camelot & SEGA · 1992

Shining Force is the game that taught a whole generation what a tactical RPG was before anyone in the West had a name for the genre. Thirty units, a grid, terrain that matters, and a battle system honest enough that you can lose a fight and know exactly which of your decisions caused it.

It's also a game that trusts you with almost no information. It knows the exact order in which everyone is going to act — agility rolls decide it at the start of every round — and it simply doesn't tell you. It knows how much experience you just wasted by levelling up at 99 EXP, and it says nothing. It tracks nothing about the soldiers you've carried through twenty battles.

Most of what lives in this room is me putting that information back on the screen.
