Latest from the workshop

Shining Force — Turn Queue & Tactics v13

A quality-of-life romhack for the US Mega Drive release. It puts the turn order on screen, lets you pick a difficulty when you start a new game, stops the game from throwing away surplus experience, and keeps a tally of every kill and every death for each member of the Force. Roughly three kilobytes of IPS patch, with not a single original byte moved.

About

I play role-playing games and I write code, which is a combination that sooner or later leads you to open one of those games up and start moving instructions around inside it.

This is where everything that comes out of that ends up: the mods I build, notes on how these games are put together, thoughts on the ones I keep going back to, and the occasional long argument with myself about why a thirty-year-old strategy RPG still feels better than most things released last year. Whatever the next obsession turns out to be, it gets written up here too.

The name is borrowed, not invented: Creed's Mansion is a place you visit in Shining Force II, and it seemed like the right sign to hang over a house that collects old games.

Everything published here is either my own work or clearly credited. Patches are distributed as IPS files: bring your own ROM — no ROMs are hosted here, and they shouldn't be hosted anywhere else either.
